# Product Roadmap

## Included in this ZIP
- Premium responsive discovery homepage.
- Hindi / English / Hinglish interface labels.
- 12 departments and 72 focus areas.
- Search and feed filters.
- Reddit, GitHub, Hugging Face, Hacker News, OpenAlex and RSS connector groups.
- Scheduled scan orchestration and source health API.
- Student Universe, Skill Roadmaps and Build & Launch Academy.
- No public admin interface.

## Next production upgrades
1. PostgreSQL persistence and migrations.
2. Field-level pricing/status verification with official product pages.
3. Dedicated official company changelog and public roadmap adapters.
4. Full translation of long-form discovery descriptions, not only interface labels.
5. User accounts, saved discoveries and progress sync (with privacy controls).
6. Source-specific image metadata and image licensing checks.
7. Automated test suite, monitoring and backup policy.
8. Better community relevance scoring and moderator/source policy review.
