# Source Policy

1. Prefer official product pages, changelogs, documentation and public APIs.
2. Reddit and Hacker News are community discovery signals, not authoritative product facts.
3. Keep original source URLs visible.
4. Do not label a product Free unless a reliable source supports that claim.
5. Keep pricing, licence, beta and launch status separate.
6. Do not invent dates, features, availability or performance claims.
7. Remove broken, duplicate, unsafe or misleading entries.
8. Respect source terms, rate limits and robots/access rules.
